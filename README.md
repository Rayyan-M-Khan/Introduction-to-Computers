# Introduction-to-Computers

Computers are an electronic device having hardwares RAM , CPU and graphic cards.

They also contain many softwares for user requirements.

They are used for multitasking and making work , complex calculations easier for human beings.

## Types Of Computers

There are many types of computers iam describing only 2 of them:

1 . **SuperComputers:** These Computers are extremely powerful and are used for complex calculationns 

2 . **MainframeComputers:** These large Computers are used by banks and mostly Hospitals for bulk data processing.

### History Of Computers
There are Five Generation of Computers I am describing 1 , 2 and 5th Generation that are current computers:

1 . **First Generation(1940-1950):** They were really big and large in size and used vaccum tubes.

2 . **Second Generation (1950–1960):** They were slightly smaller then first generation computers and more relaible. Used Transistors instead of vaccum tubes

3 . **Fifth Generation Current generation AI:** Highly compact and small devices easy to use and most reliable then any other generations computers.
